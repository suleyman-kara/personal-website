PathGuard came together very quickly. My friend Aysima Çil invited me onto her team, and we entered the TEKNOFEST 2026 AI in Healthcare competition together. The project was a machine learning pipeline that classifies genetic variants as pathogenic or benign.

## We used AI a lot

It was our first project, and we leaned heavily on AI tools while building it. For a first project I think that is the right call: someone has to teach you and show you how. When that person is not next to you, AI does the job.

We scored 92/100 in the preliminary evaluation and advanced to the semi-finals.

## Where it fell short

Our work was still amateurish, and past a certain point that cost us points. We were eliminated in the semi-finals and did not make the final.

What I took from it: AI runs out at some point. Somewhere along the way your own taste has to come in. You have to really understand the subject and put forward your own idea based on that. The tool moves you forward from the starting line, but the part that makes the difference is still yours.
