Aysima and I applied to Robotistan's Arduino UNO Q Türkiye Innovation Project Competition with two separate ideas. Same team, different ideas.

## My idea: fall detection over Wi-Fi

Mine started as a glasses idea and then turned into something else: using Wi-Fi signals to tell whether elderly people have fallen and how they are doing. Recent work has shown that routers can track people's movement, and that is where the inspiration came from.

The scenario I had in mind was a care home. With routers placed in the rooms, and with no cameras and nothing for anyone to wear, it would be possible to tell how people are doing and whether there are signs of something going wrong.

That idea was not accepted.

## The idea that got in: Suİz

We turned to an agriculture idea. Suİz is an irrigation system running on the Arduino UNO Q: it decides when and how much to water not only from soil moisture but also from the water budget that is left.

Aysima's effort carried the application. It turned out to be a really strong one, and we were selected among the top 100 teams to compete.

## Now

I am building the prototype with Aysima Çil and Hilal Pusa. Details and progress are on the [project page](https://berceste-team.github.io/Su-z/).
